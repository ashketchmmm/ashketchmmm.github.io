The final project for my 6.4550 Interactive Music Systems class was a game called "The Wrong Key." The idea was to create a musically inclined platformer where the main character can change "genres" by changing the soundtrack. They have to explore an old family home and solve an ancient curse, while switching between a haunted and normal version of the house. I was one of two programmers for the project, coding most of the main character's gameplay logic, half of the music puzzles, and most of the collision and item interaction logic.
