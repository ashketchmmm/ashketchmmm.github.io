As part of the "Investigation of Subterranean Features in the Appalachian Region" Marshall University REU, I worked with two other students to create a game and manuscript to educate and advocate for green urban infrastructure and its benefits with Dr. Mehmet Kosa. Our project was developed in Unreal Engine 5 to enhance the realism of the urban infrastructure and due to the readily available, free assets we could utilize. The project took place from May 2024 to January 2025, and our final draft is linked above.
