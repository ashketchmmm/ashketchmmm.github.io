For our final project in "Creating Video Games", my team created Trash Bandits, a game about a cat and raccoon stealing trash. 
I served as one of two main programmers for the project, focused on creating the logic for the trash cans, the raccoon's mechanics, 
and the movement for the cat. I also helped with the overall game design for the project and the characters, worked with the other 
programmer to use a 3D base for better navmesh navigation, and created hitboxes for the furniture and trash cans.
