_PartyUp_ was the final project for my 6.4500 Design for the Web class. The project was to create a chat app that addressed some kind of need, and I 
chose to address physical gamers who have to play online, such as during the pandemic or due to distance. _PartyUp_ was prototyped with a few games in 
mind, such as D&D and Catan, with in-house tools to support those. 

Players could also create different profiles, helpful for TTRPGs, and can reference the game's rulebook as well. 
The chat app also supports replies, in-chat direct messaging, and different profile pictures for every user's distinct profile.
