Currently, as an MEng student, I am creating a thesis focused on how games can have an impact on education and career aspirations. 
I have scraped games off the Steam Web API based on relevant tags and identifiers related to engineering, education, and science to analyze thousands of comments. 
The data collected will help inform the design scheme and scope of a manufacturing-focused game intended to encourage interests in young students towards 
manufacturing or production as a career option.

As an undergraduate, I developed and optimized XR and non-XR simulations and polishing previous UI/UX experiences. I also 
contributed to software architecture and creating reusable software components for simulations.
