My teammates and I created a game called Spellstrike, where two wizards duel 
to the death. The game is a simple turn-based, deck builder where the two players 
pick a location to move to, a card to play, and can get power-ups that double a 
card's effect. 

I served as the main programmer for this project. I coded most of the gameplay logic, excluding the wall logic. 
The gameplay logic was fleshed out using manager scripts that oversaw turn logic, deck randomization, and 
health and game state logic.
