For our final project, my teammate and I created a short game called "You vs. Liquid," where the player is a shapeshifting orb that can change between three different types of materials. Each material, glass, plastic, and metal, has a differing density, mass, and speed. The goal of the game is puzzle completion and level traversal, as the player must reach a goalpost using their mechanics. Realistically-simulated liquids were added to the game using SPH simulations by my teammate, with my focus being implementation of player and material mechanics.
